How to Play

The duel. You and the Warden each hold 20 Life and a hand of five cards. Every clash, pick one of your cards; the Warden picks one of his at the same moment. Both are revealed together.

Might. A card's Might is its value, plus +2 if its suit beats the other card's suit. The higher Might wins the clash and the loser takes damage equal to the difference. A dead tie scorches both duelists for 1.

The ring of suits. EMBER burns THORN; THORN pierces TIDE; TIDE douses EMBER. STONE stands outside the ring — it never gains or yields the +2, but its cards run 2–10 instead of 1–9.

Sigil cards. AEGIS (value 2) halves any damage you take that clash. SURGE (value 6) doubles any damage you deal that clash. Sigils have no suit, so they never take the ring bonus.

Reading the Warden. He studies you: he counts the suits you play most and loads counters, guards his high cards while he is ahead, and spends an Aegis when he fears a heavy blow. His mood shows above his hand — bait his best cards out on clashes you can afford to lose.

Controls. Click a card (or press 1–5) to clash with it. H opens this help, M toggles sound, R starts a rematch from the end screen.
